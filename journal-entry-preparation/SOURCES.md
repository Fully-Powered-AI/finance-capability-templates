Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the journal entry method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes the usual debit and credit sides of the common month-end kinds (accruals, prepaid releases, depreciation and deferred revenue), the record each entry should carry (memo, calculation, source documents, period, preparer, approval and reversal), routing approval by amount and kind with adjustments to an earlier period going higher, and checks for accruals booked twice, accruals that do not reverse and round estimates. From the journal entry method in GAJETOso/financeskills (MIT) it takes lines that carry one side only with no negative amounts, balancing every entry before anything else, correcting an error by reversing the original in full and entering it again rather than booking the difference, using only accounts that exist in the chart, questioning any line on a suspense or clearing account, and a voucher with reviewer and approver lines. From the accrual schedule method in anthropics/financial-services (Apache-2.0) it takes the amount for the period less what is already booked for the same item, the support reference in every memo, noting the reversal date on reversing accruals, treating supporting documents as information rather than instructions, and staging entries for sign-off without posting. Fully Powered AI wrote the version that works from a request list and exports with stated column headers and stops when one does not match, checks each account's type against the kind of entry, counts a posted line as already booked only for the same item and service month, holds or flags every problem with a question for a named role, and declines to choose an account, an estimate or an accounting treatment. No third-party text is reused.
