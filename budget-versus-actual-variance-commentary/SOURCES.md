Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the variance method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes splitting a line's movement into a volume part and a price part that must add back to the whole, commenting on a line when either an amount or a percentage limit is passed, giving each commented line what is expected next, and a bridge from budget to actual that has to reconcile. From the variance method in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) it takes reconciling the totals before any line is examined, which here means adding the lines to the export's own totals, the rule for when revenue and cost lines are favourable or unfavourable, flagging a line that has spending and no budget, gathering the lines under the limit into one figure, showing amounts that pull in opposite directions rather than only their net, and marking figures that come from outside the ledger. Both methods put the largest movements first and refuse explanations that only repeat the movement. Fully Powered AI added the default export headers with a stop when they do not match, the floor for small lines, the rule that only a line's owner can confirm its explanation, with every other explanation marked to be confirmed and none supplied by the assistant, the checks for notes that disagree with the figures, and the questions for each owner. Nothing is changed or sent. No third-party text is reused.
