Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the bank and petty cash reconciliation method in skills-agents-co/skills-and-agents-library (MIT) it takes one account and one period per run; a match on amount, payee and a date window counted in business days, with the window stated on the result; putting both sides on one sign convention before comparing; showing each side's own description of a matched pair; leaving unpaired any line that has more than one equally good candidate; comparing the ending balances instead of trusting the matches; and leaving the bank and the books untouched. From the account reconciliation method in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) it takes matching on reference numbers as well as amount and date; sorting every unmatched item into a kind; ageing each open item in brackets; the two-sided proof in which the adjusted balances must agree with nothing added to force them; carrying last period's open items forward and ageing them again; flagging deposits that linger and payments outstanding too long; and the preparer and reviewer sign-off. Fully Powered AI wrote the version that works from two exports with stated column headers, foots each export against its own balances before any matching, matches by reference first and by amount and date second, keeps unpaired lines in the proof on their own side, lists entries for approval and posts nothing, and turns every open question into one sentence for a named role. No third-party text is reused.
