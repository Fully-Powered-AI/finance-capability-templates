From the thirteen-week forecast method in GTMify/aigtm (MIT) it takes the week-by-week grid of receipts and payments by kind with closing cash for each week, counting only sales the owner has won and never hoped-for deals, checking for payments owners often leave out such as tax estimates and insurance, never estimating a figure the owner did not give (payroll above all), the lowest point with its week and cause, how long cash lasts with no new sales, flagging weeks below one payroll or below zero, testing what happens when the largest customers pay late, a one-line view of where the business stands, leaving financing advice to the owner and their advisers, rolling the forecast forward each week, and having a professional check the figures before the business takes on debt, adds staff or pays money out to the owners. From the cash-flow-snapshot method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes never assuming an opening balance and always stating its date, timing each invoice by that customer's own payment history with a fallback when the history is thin, showing an export's headers and asking when they are unclear, leaving out invoices with nothing left to pay, spotting regular payments recorded as separate bills from the same supplier at a similar amount three months running and asking before counting them, counting card spending not yet in the books, naming every source used and every one not supplied, and treating a blank as unknown rather than zero. Fully Powered AI added the numbered assumptions printed on the forecast with the weeks each one affects, the checks that prove the arithmetic and account for every export row, the rule that an invoice past its usual timing is not counted until the owner confirms a date, the case that removes committed sales together with the purchases they need, the decisions written as facts with no recommendation, and the rule that nothing is paid, moved or sent.
