Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the external audit preparation method in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) it takes turning the auditors' request list into a tracker with an owner, a status and an internal date set a few working days ahead of the auditors' date; noting the form the auditors want each document in; comparing the list with last year's for new, changed and dropped requests; explaining balances that moved by more than a set threshold; sending each expected question to whoever holds the answer; review by a second person before anything is handed over; and setting last year's findings beside what was done about them. Fully Powered AI added the control evidence part of the pack, from its own brief and common audit practice: the controls list with the evidence each control leaves, the check of who approves, records, handles the money and checks in each process, and the check of payments at or above the approval limit. It also added the stated column headers with a stop when one does not match, draft answers built only from the facts supplied with their source named, questions on legal claims, tax positions, suspected fraud and the business's ability to keep operating left undrafted for a named person and the company's advisers, the numbered decisions for named roles, and the rule that nothing goes to the auditors until a person approves it.
