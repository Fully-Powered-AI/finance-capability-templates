# Weekly receivables summary

The finance lead takes one page into each week’s management meeting: what came in, where every receivable stands, which exceptions are open and who owns them, and the decisions management must make. Every figure ties to the aging report.

*Workflow template · Finance and accounting · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/weekly-receivables-summary/)*

## The outcome

Management sees receivables every week, in the same shape, on one page. The finance lead walks in knowing the cash that arrived, how each bracket moved and why, and whose desk each open exception sits on. The page ends with the decisions only management can make, each with its facts.

Month end stops being the first time anyone hears a client has gone past 60 days; the summary flags it the week before. The finance lead edits and approves the draft and decides what goes to management.

## Who starts the work, and when

The billing coordinator or the controller runs it at the end of the weekly receivables cycle, after the exception list and follow-up plan are done. The finance lead reviews it the same day and presents it at the next management meeting.

## What they supply

- This week’s and last week’s aging reports.
- The week’s receipts log, showing what was applied and what is held, and the invoices issued.
- The exception list, with who acts and who decides on each item.
- The follow-up plan: drafts due, holds and invoices not chased.
- Last week’s summary, to carry open decisions forward.

## What comes out

One page in a fixed order, with a tie-out line that proves the totals: last week’s open balance, plus invoices issued, minus cash applied, equals this week’s. If it does not tie, nothing is drafted until the billing coordinator finds why.

## What has to change for your company

Decide who reads the page and on which day. Set your brackets and the movement size worth reporting. Agree which decisions go to management and which stay with the controller, such as credits above a set amount or pausing work for a client. Fix the section order once. Make the aging report the single source: when the summary and the ledger disagree, the ledger wins.
