# Weekly receivables summary: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Complete weekly summary at an invented 60-person professional-services firm.

**Weekly receivables summary: week of 21 September 2026**

- **For:** Finance lead, for the management meeting on Monday 28 September. Receipts 21 to 24 September; aging as of 25 September.
- **What came in:** Five receipts, \$16,100. Applied by the billing coordinator: \$10,150, to 1038 and 1040 (Client B), 1047 (Client G) and \$1,000 of 1042 (Client A). Held unapplied: \$5,950 in two receipts, both with the controller.
- **Aging by bracket:** Current \$8,900 (last week \$13,500). 1 to 30 days \$11,400 (\$9,900). 31 to 60 days \$12,050 (\$10,200). 61 to 90 days and over 90: none. Total open \$32,350 (\$33,600). Overdue \$23,450, of which \$3,800 is disputed.
- **Movements:** Paid: 1038, 1040, 1047. Reduced: 1042, from \$1,200 to \$200. Issued: 1051, Client H, \$8,900. Moved bracket: 1036 into 31 to 60 days; 1044 and 1045 into 1 to 30 days. Tie-out: \$33,600 + \$8,900 − \$10,150 = \$32,350.
- **Exceptions open:** Four. 1042, \$200: billing coordinator asking Client A. \$5,600 wire: billing coordinator with the bank. \$350 from Client J: Client J’s account lead. 1035, \$3,800 disputed: Client E’s account lead. The controller decides each.
- **Follow-ups due:** 1031, Client C, \$6,400: from the account lead, finance lead copied. 1036, Client G, \$1,850: from the billing coordinator, account lead copied. Held: 1042, 1044, 1045. Not chased: 1035.
- **Before month end:** 1031 enters 61 to 90 days on 28 September. Once the controller decides where the wire belongs and the billing coordinator applies it to 1044 or 1045, 1 to 30 days falls to \$5,800.
- **Decisions for management:** Two. 1. Whether new work for Client C continues while 1031 is unpaid. 2. Whether Client E’s account lead may propose a resolution on 1035, including a credit, which the controller would then issue.

Every figure traces to the aging report as of 25 September and the receipts log for 21 to 24 September. The summary changes no records and sends nothing.
