# Investor update

Each month or quarter, the founder gets a drafted update for investors: the headline, key figures against plan and last period, cash and runway, wins, problems told plainly with what is being done, and specific asks. Every figure comes from the company's own reports. The founder approves and sends it.

*Workflow template · Finance and accounting · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/investor-update/)*

## The outcome

Your investors hear from you on time, in the same shape every period, and they hear the hard news from you first. The draft opens with your headline, then the same standing figures as last time, each set against plan and against the last period, so a reader sees at once what moved. Cash and runway are worked out from your cash report, with money from investors kept out of the burn and funding you expect but have not banked shown on its own line.

Every figure that is well behind plan lands under Problems, with your explanation and what you are doing about it, or with a plain marker where your explanation is still missing. Last period's problems and asks are followed up, so investors see that what you raised was handled. Each ask is checked for what you want, from whom and by when, because a named kind of person and a date give an investor something to do.

Gaps come first, as questions: a headline that skips the period's worst number, a figure that differs from the one you sent last time, an ask with no deadline or no clear target. The workflow template prepares. You write the words that matter, settle the questions, and send the update yourself.

## Who starts the work, and when

The founder or CEO starts it, or whoever assembles the update for them: the finance lead, a chief of staff or an operations lead. They run it on the same day each month or quarter, once the period's billing, sales and cash reports are final and the founder has written a few notes, and again after the questions are settled.

Between regular updates, when news should not wait, it drafts a shorter note with the news, cash and runway, and any asks.

## What they supply

- **The figures:** an export of the standing metrics, each with this period, last period, plan, which direction is better and the report it came from.
- **The cash report** for the last three months: opening and closing cash, funding received, other money in and money out.
- **The founder's notes:** the headline, wins, problems with their causes and what is being done, news on what the last update raised, asks and team changes.
- **The last update as sent,** to follow up on what it raised and check last period's figures.
- **Your settings:** the period, the greeting and sign-off, and the thresholds you want flagged.

No accounting system, bank or mailbox is connected, and no password is ever asked for. Everything is pasted or attached by a person.

## What comes out

1. **Questions to settle,** for the founder or the finance lead, each naming the section it affects.
2. **The update itself,** ready to approve: headline, the numbers, cash and runway, wins, problems and what is being done, since the last update, asks and team, ending with the founder's sign-off.
3. **Checks for the founder:** the word count, where every figure came from, any figure that is the founder's own and in no report, and anything left out and why.

Good looks like this: a reader who stops after the headline knows the period's best and worst news, and every number in the update can be traced to a report in under a minute.

## What has to change for your company

Choose your standing metrics and their order once, and keep them; the workflow template shows every one every period. Map your reports' column headings to the ones it expects, or keep its defaults. Set the variance from plan that counts as well behind, the runway that should always be raised as a problem, and how long the update may be. Agree how your finance lead counts net burn, which customers may be named and who gives that permission, and where the sent update is kept so the next one can follow it up. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini; decide which reports the assistant may be given.
