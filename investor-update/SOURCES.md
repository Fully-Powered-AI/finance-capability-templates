# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two openly licensed methods for investor communication shaped it. From LeadMagic/gtm-skills (MIT): an update with the same sections every period (a headline, key metrics, wins, challenges, asks, team changes, and cash and runway every time); writing problems honestly and not skipping a hard month; asks specific enough for an investor to act on; keeping the update short; and a separate note to investors when serious news cannot wait. From beingsmit/technical-product-gtm (MIT): setting every figure against plan, keeping the same metrics every period and never dropping one that looks bad, giving bad news with what happened, its cause, its effect and what is being done, following up in each later update on problems raised earlier, keeping the burn and runway story apart from the revenue story, and telling investors about major news within days rather than at the regular date. Neither package's board-meeting structure, crisis playbook, fundraising narrative, scripts or fixed list of software-company metrics is used. Fully Powered AI's own: the export read under named headers with a stop on any mismatch; the cash report tied out month by month before anything is drafted; net burn that leaves out investor and lender money, averaged over three months, with runway rounded down and expected funding kept off the figure; the check of last period's figures against the update actually sent; the rule that every metric well behind plan must appear under Problems with the founder's cause or a marker saying it is missing; the headline check; founder's figures labelled apart from reported ones; customers named only with permission and departures without reasons; round terms sent to the company's advisers; and the rule that the founder writes the words that matter and sends the update. No third-party text is reused.
