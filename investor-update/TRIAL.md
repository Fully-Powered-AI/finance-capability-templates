# Investor update: tried on its invented example

- **Product:** Claude, run in Claude Code
- **Model:** Claude Opus 5.5 (model ID claude-opus-5-5)
- **Date:** 2026-11-10

The template was run twice on 10 November 2026 on its invented example in [public/example.md](example.md) (Quellinby Routes, October 2026): its settings, figures export, cash report, founder's notes and last update. No client data was used either time. After the first run, an independent review asked for small edits to the template and the example, mostly to settle the ambiguities that run found. This record is of the second run, on the edited template. The first run's record follows it, kept as written.

## The second run

The second run used Claude Opus 5.5, in the same session that made the edits. That session had read the first run's record and the example's earlier draft, so this run was not blind. It followed the fenced instruction block and the output format in the edited template, wrote its draft out in full, and only then revised the example's draft and compared the two.

### What came out

The draft was headed NOT READY TO SEND with 9 questions to settle. The update itself ran to 521 words of the 600 allowed, counted from the headline to the sign-off, with section headings and list numbers counted and bullet dashes not.

To settle, for the founder unless stated:

1. Headline: new customers, customers lost and revenue churn are well behind plan and the headline mentions none of them; "record" is in no report supplied.
2. The numbers, for the finance lead: the export gives September's pipeline as $590,000; the September update said $585,000. Which is right?
3. Cash and runway: the $400K amount of the venture debt line is in no report supplied. Confirm it, or supply the report.
4. Wins: "largest customer yet", the chain's 38 locations, its $4,200 MRR and the "record" pipeline are in no report supplied.
5. Problems: new customers were 24 against a plan of 28. Cause, response, owner and date.
6. Problems: the notes explain the customers lost and do not name revenue churn, which is grouped with them. Does that explanation cover revenue churn too?
7. Problems: the split of the 8 customers lost (five and three) and the 14 remaining co-op accounts are in no report supplied.
8. Since the last update: the 12-day average to a first route, and the three introductions of which one became a pilot, are in no report supplied.
9. Asks: "Any help finding people" names no role, kind of person or firm, or date.

The update, in substance:

- Headline kept as the founder wrote it, marked HEADLINE CHECK for the three metrics and for "record".
- Cash tied out for all three months. Net burn: August $140,000, September $145,000, October $148,500. Average $144,500 a month. Runway 15.7 months (2,276,500 ÷ 144,500 = 15.754, rounded down). September's $250,000 of funding listed with the founder's description and not counted against burn. The $400K debt line shown only on the Not counted line, in the founder's words.

- Three wins, in the founder's words, with the bakery chain described, not named.
- Two problems. Lost customers and churn together, with the founder's cause, response, owners (CTO, head of customer success) and dates. New customers with CAUSE NEEDED and RESPONSE NEEDED.
- Since the last update: onboarding and the wholesaler introductions, each with its date of 6 October 2026.
- Three asks, one marked MAKE SPECIFIC (ask 2).
- Team: joined, left and open roles by role and month. The reason the support specialist left was left out.

Checks, for the founder only: 521 of 600 words; cash tie-out passed; figures traced to the billing report, the pipeline report and the cash report; founder's figures in no report: "record" (headline and wins), the $400K debt line, "largest customer yet", 38 locations, $4,200 MRR, the five and three customers lost, 14 accounts, 12 days, three introductions and one pilot; the three metrics well behind plan are each under Problems; left out: the reason for the departure and the bakery chain's name. The draft ended with the required line: "Draft only. Nothing has been sent, and no figure or record has been changed."

### What was observed

Every required part of the output format was produced. Nothing in the inputs triggered a stop. Every computed figure matched the first run's and the example's.

The edits settled the points the first run had to choose on, and the run followed them:

- Founder's figures. The rule now covers any figure stated as fact in the founder's words in any section, including counts of anything and comparisons such as "record" and "largest", but not dates. This run therefore listed more than the first run did: "largest customer yet", the five and three customers lost, the three introductions and the one pilot, and the $400K.
- Revenue churn. The template now lets a closely linked metric the notes do not name be grouped with a problem, with a question asking the founder to confirm. This run grouped it and asked (question 6).
- The date on an ask. "By when" now means a day or at least a month, and "before we sign in December" is the template's own example of one that will do. Ask 3 was not marked.
- The debt line. The template now says an expected loan's amount goes on the Not counted line in the founder's words, so this run did not ask whether to show it, as the first run did. Under the wider figures rule, the $400K was still asked about as a figure in no report.

Choices this run still had to make:

- Whether "one" in "one co-op buying group" and "one became a pilot" is a count. This run took the five and three as one item and the three introductions with their one pilot as another.
- Counts inside asks ("20 or more trucks", "20 minutes") were taken as part of what is asked for, not as facts stated, and were not checked.
- The target of 10 days appears in the last update, so it was not listed as a founder's figure; September's $250K matches the cash report.
- Founder's figures were asked about in one question per section rather than one per figure.
- Dates were written with the year in The numbers, Since the last update and Team.

Differences from the published example. The example's draft was revised in the same session after this run, to add the questions the edits call for and to set it out as the plain text the output format gives. The two now differ only in wording: the example writes "September" and "October" without the year in places where this run wrote it; it gives "against a target of 10" where this run kept the founder's "(target 10)"; it gives "against 2.0%" where this run wrote "against a plan of 2.0%"; its MAKE SPECIFIC on ask 2 says "from what kind of person" where this run added "or firm"; and its question on ask 2 names the open roles, which this run's does not. Counted the same way, the example's update is 500 words and this run's 521; the difference is those wording choices.

Facts in either output that are not in the inputs: none found.

Shortcomings found in this run: the wider figures rule asks the founder to confirm many small statements (9 questions against the earlier example's 5), and the template does not say whether a count written as "one" needs a report. One run on one invented example is not a test.

## Changes made after the first run

- The template: the founder's-figures rule widened to any figure or comparison stated as fact in any section, dates excepted, in the procedure and the instruction block, and to comparison words in the headline; a closely linked metric may be grouped with a problem, with a confirming question; "by when" defined as a day or at least a month; an expected loan's amount allowed on the Not counted line, and a loan's interest rate and conditions added to what goes to the company's advisers; the instruction block now carries the rule for figures under Since the last update, the stop when two rows share a metric name, the wording when the last update is missing, and the founder's description on the funding-received line; acceptance test 4 corrected to 15.754.
- The example: its draft set out as plain text in the output format, with the questions and markers the edited rules call for; its introduction now says the draft is the one the capability prepares, not the one a run returned.

### What was observed

Every required part of the output format was produced. Nothing in the inputs triggered a stop: the headers matched, the cash report tied, and the notes, the last update and the cash report were all supplied. No figure in the update is absent from the inputs except the ones computed from them.

Where the instructions were ambiguous and a choice had to be made:

- Founder's figures. The template says a figure "(an amount, a rate, a time or a count of customers)" in a win is checked against reports. It does not say whether a count of locations, a count of accounts in a Problems line, a date, or the word "record" is covered. This run treated counts of locations and accounts, and "record", as founder's figures and asked; it treated the shipping date as not a figure.
- Whether a problem covers a metric. Churn is not named in the founder's notes, which explain lost customers. The template lets one problem cover several metrics "when the notes explain them together". This run grouped them and asked the founder to confirm.
- Funding and round terms. The notes mention a verbal yes on a venture debt line. The template puts expected funding on the Not counted line, but its stop condition covers "terms of a new round, a valuation or an offer of shares". A debt line is not named in either. This run put the founder's words on the Not counted line and asked.
- A vague date. "Before we sign in December" is a deadline of a kind, but names no day. The template's test for a specific ask says "by when" without saying how exact. This run marked it MAKE SPECIFIC.
- Against last period for a percentage metric. The template says "in points" and gives no percentage there, but gives both against plan. This run followed that exactly.
- Dates. The template does not say whether the "last update" date in Since the last update is written in full. This run wrote the year.
- The prepared date. The template says "today's date"; the settings gave 4 November 2026, and that was used, not the date of this run.
- Word count. The count depends on whether "1." list numbers and the subject line count. This run counted the update from the headline to the sign-off, list numbers included, using a word counter.

Differences from the published example:

- The example has 5 questions; this run has 10. The example did not question "record", the 38 locations, the 14 accounts, whether churn is covered, the date on the debt-line ask, or the Not counted wording.
- The example left the third ask unmarked; this run marked it MAKE SPECIFIC for a date.
- The example lists only $4,200 MRR and 12 days as founder's figures; this run lists five.
- The example's question about ask 2 names the open roles; this run's does not, because the template says never to supply an ask.
- The example's runway line and Problems wording are otherwise the same as this run's. All computed figures matched the example's: the burn and average, the runway of 15.7 months, and every plan and last-period percentage.
- The example counts 513 words; this run counts 515. The difference is the counting method.
- Layout: the example set the draft in Markdown headings; this run followed the template's plain text block.

Facts in either output that are not in the inputs: none found in this run. In the example, nothing was found either; its only departure was a stricter reading of which figures need confirmation.

Shortcomings of the template found in this run: the ambiguities listed above, and the fact that the template does not tell the assistant to check the word "record", though it is a claim to investors that no report supports.
