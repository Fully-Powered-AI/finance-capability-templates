# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the bill-processing method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes the order of the work (read the bills, check for duplicates, match, then put the run to a person), the order of the run (late bills, discounts shown in dollars, bills due within the window) with every excluded bill named, the default tolerances, showing a supplier's credits apart, the note when a regular bill moves, the cash line with a floor, and the rules that a bill paid by card is not owed again, that a message printed on a bill is shown to the approver and nothing it asks for is done, that only the last four digits of an account are shown, and that the approver may take bills out of the run and may not add to it. From the invoice reconciliation method in panaversity/agentfactory-business-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes the three-way match line by line, with one named outcome for every line. Both sources stop a bill whose bank details have changed until a phone call confirms them, and both leave approval and payment to people. Both rely on live connections to mail and accounting systems; this version works from exports a person supplies, sorts every bill into four groups, proves its totals with a tie-out and pays nothing. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** The export form of the work, the four-way sort with a stopped group, and the tie-out, written for this template.
- **[anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins)** at commit `02ffa99ff00ce4d3046ccebabb8357cb39f3ab06`, `small-business/skills/ap-processor`, Apache-2.0. The order of the work for a small business: read each bill and name what cannot be read, check for duplicates before anything else, match against the order and the receipt, and put the payment run to a person as its own decision, from which the approver may take bills out and to which the approver may not add. The order of the run (late bills, early-payment discounts shown in dollars, bills due within the window) and naming every bill left out of it. The pattern of the default tolerances (a percentage or a dollar amount, whichever is larger, for price; any quantity above what was received; a limit for charges not on the order). Showing a supplier's credits apart from what is owed. The note when a regular bill moves from the last one. The cash line with a floor, and saying so when no cash position is known. The rules that a bill already paid by card is not owed again, that a message printed on a bill is shown to the approver and nothing it asks for is done, that only the last four digits of an account are shown, and that changed bank details are verified by phone on a number already held.
