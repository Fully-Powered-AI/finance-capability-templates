# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The shape of the work draws on three published close methods: the close stages and sign-off order in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0), the day-by-day sequence and the idea of tasks that wait on other tasks in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0), and the working-day calendar, the chain that sets the close length and the status list in GAJETOso/financeskills (MIT). Fully Powered AI narrowed the work to one checklist built from your own task list, added the dated calendar that skips your closed days, the flags for a missing owner, an absent owner, an order that cannot work and a person reviewing their own work, and the rule that entries are prepared for approval and never posted. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Fully Powered AI's own rules for prepared work: the organization's own list as the only authority, every gap written as a question for a named role, and a person who approves, posts and locks.
- **[CaseMark/skills](https://github.com/CaseMark/skills)** at commit `afe48c44914d753297766404378e871d1f88d7a8`, `skills/finance/executing-month-end-close`, Apache-2.0. The stages of a close in order (cut-off, entries, reconciliations, comparison with the prior period, the pack, sign-off, lock) and the check, before the period is locked, of which tasks are not yet complete. No text is reused. Its licence notice at that commit is in [notices/CaseMark-skills-LICENSE.txt](notices/CaseMark-skills-LICENSE.txt).
- **[anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins)** at commit `02ffa99ff00ce4d3046ccebabb8357cb39f3ab06`, `finance/skills/close-management`, Apache-2.0. The day-by-day arrangement of close work counted from the period end, the idea that each task waits on named earlier tasks, and a status for each task with a note on what blocks it. No text is reused. Its licence notice at that commit is in [notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt](notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt).

Counting the close in working days, finding the dependent chain that sets how long the close takes, and raising a late or blocked task on that chain the same day. No text is reused. Its licence notice at that commit is in [notices/GAJETOso-financeskills-LICENSE.txt](notices/GAJETOso-financeskills-LICENSE.txt).
