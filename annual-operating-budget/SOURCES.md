From the budgeting-cycle method in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) it takes collecting a submission from each budget owner, asking for a reason wherever a line moves sharply from this year, listing the submissions that have not arrived, checking people costs against the approved staffing plan, checking that the totals agree at every level, and handing the gaps to leadership before anything is approved.
