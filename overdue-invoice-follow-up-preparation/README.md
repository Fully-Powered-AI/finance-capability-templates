# Overdue-invoice follow-up preparation

Every overdue invoice gets a follow-up drafted in your firm’s tone for its age and addressed from the right person, and every invoice that should not be chased is held with the reason written down.

*Workflow template · Finance and accounting · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/overdue-invoice-follow-up-preparation/)*

## The outcome

Collections stop depending on who remembers to chase. Each week, every overdue invoice is either ready to follow up or held for a stated reason. The drafts read like your firm wrote them: a light reminder at 10 days, a request for a date at 40, a direct note from the account lead once a promise is missed. Each cites the invoice, the amount, the due date and what the client last said.

The plan also holds back what should not go out: the disputed invoice, the one a still-unidentified payment may have settled, the balance whose cause nobody has confirmed. Nothing is sent by the workflow template. The account lead or billing coordinator reads each draft, edits it and sends it.

## Who starts the work, and when

The billing coordinator runs it each week after aging is refreshed and the exception list is assembled, which keeps every hold current. The billing coordinator sends the early reminders. Account leads review and send the firmer notes for their own clients.

## What they supply

- The refreshed aging report.
- Account-lead notes: promised dates, disputes, holds, preferred contacts.
- Past correspondence on each overdue invoice, to avoid repeating a reminder or ignoring a reply.
- The current exception list, which supplies holds for unconfirmed balances and unidentified receipts.
- The firm’s tone guide: brackets, one approved message for each, and who sends.

## What comes out

A follow-up plan listing every overdue invoice with its action and sender, then the drafts. Each held invoice carries its reason and who can release it.

## What has to change for your company

Set your brackets and one approved message for each. Decide who sends and who is copied at each bracket, and whether a missed promise goes straight to the account lead. Agree the holds: disputed, on hold by agreement, balance under review, payment unidentified. Decide where drafts land. No draft offers a payment plan, a discount or a credit; the sender adds one only with the controller’s approval.
