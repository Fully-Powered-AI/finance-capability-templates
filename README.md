# Fully Powered Workflow Templates: Finance

A finance team that knows where every dollar stands before anyone asks. These workflow templates take the recurring work of finance and accounting, such as receivables, collections and reporting, and give an AI assistant a precise way to prepare it, so your people spend their time on the decisions.

This repository holds 17 workflow templates for finance and accounting teams. Each one is a single piece of work with its own folder: what it is for and who starts it, the complete template an AI assistant follows, a worked example with invented data, what to change for your company, what has been checked, and its sources.

## The workflow templates

- **[Annual operating budget](annual-operating-budget/)**: For an owner or finance lead preparing next year: revenue and costs by month and by team, built from this year's figures and each budget owner's drivers, every assumption written down, capital purchases listed apart, the whole checked against cash, and the questions leadership has to settle.
- **[Audit preparation](audit-preparation/)**: Before the external auditors arrive, your controller holds one audit pack: every request with its owner, date, document and status, the control evidence they will test, last year's findings and what was done, and draft answers to the questions they will ask. People approve everything before it goes.
- **[Bank reconciliation preparation](bank-reconciliation-preparation/)**: Before the month is signed off, your bookkeeper holds one bank reconciliation for one account: every statement line matched to the books or explained, every open item aged, a proof that the two balances agree, and the entries still to make. Nothing is posted until a person approves.
- **[Budget-versus-actual variance commentary](budget-versus-actual-variance-commentary/)**: Before the month's results go to management, the finance lead gets written commentary on every line that moved past the threshold: the figures, the budget owner's explanation marked confirmed or to be confirmed, a bridge from budget to actual, and the questions still open. No cause is invented.
- **[Creator commission statement](creator-commission-statement/)**: Each month, the finance lead gets a draft statement of what every creator has earned, worked from the store's orders export: commission under the programme's rules, flat fees, amounts carried under the minimum, and a payout list. A person approves it; finance pays.
- **[Investor update](investor-update/)**: Each month or quarter, the founder gets a drafted update for investors: the headline, key figures against plan and last period, cash and runway, wins, problems told plainly with what is being done, and specific asks. Every figure comes from the company's own reports. The founder approves and sends it.
- **[Journal entry preparation](journal-entry-preparation/)**: At month end, your bookkeeper hands the controller every proposed journal entry ready to review: balanced lines on accounts from your own chart, the arithmetic and the document behind each amount, whether it reverses, and who approves it. Nothing is posted until a person approves.
- **[Month-end close checklist](month-end-close-checklist/)**: Before the month ends, your controller holds the whole close on one page: every task with its owner, its date and what it waits on, the chain that sets the close date, and each gap already written as a question. Entries are prepared for approval, never posted.
- **[Overdue-invoice follow-up preparation](overdue-invoice-follow-up-preparation/)**: Every overdue invoice gets a follow-up drafted in your firm’s tone for its age and addressed from the right person, and every invoice that should not be chased is held with the reason written down.
- **[Payroll preparation and timesheet review](payroll-preparation-and-timesheet-review/)**: Before each payroll run, the owner gets one sheet built from the timesheet and roster exports: hours by person and type, every missing punch and odd entry raised as a question, and an estimated total. Nothing is keyed in, submitted or paid until a person approves.
- **[Price change review](price-change-review/)**: Before prices change, the owner or finance lead gets one review: the margin on each product or service from the sales and cost exports, how costs have moved, what each proposed price does to sales and gross profit across a stated range of customer responses, and dated competitor prices. It recommends no price.
- **[Receivables exception review](receivables-exception-review/)**: The controller gets every short payment, unmatched or ambiguous receipt, possible duplicate and disputed invoice on one list, each with its evidence and a proposed next step, before anything is applied on a guess.
- **[Spreadsheet check before sharing](spreadsheet-check-before-sharing/)**: Before a model or schedule goes to a client, a lender or a board, the sender gets one report on it: every error value, short total, buried assumption and hidden row pinned to its cell, each with a fix or a question, and a plain list of what was not checked. Nothing in the workbook is changed.
- **[Supplier bill review and payment-run preparation](supplier-bill-review-and-payment-run-preparation/)**: Before each payment run, the person who approves it gets one sheet: every supplier bill checked against its purchase order and what was received, duplicates and changed bank details stopped, credits shown, and a proposed run with its total. Nothing is paid until a person approves.
- **[Thirteen-week cash flow forecast](thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast/)**: The owner sees cash week by week for the next thirteen weeks: what comes in, what goes out, the lowest point and every week below the floor they set, with each assumption written on the page. The forecast informs the decision; the owner makes it, and nothing is paid or moved.
- **[Weekly receivables summary](weekly-receivables-summary/)**: The finance lead takes one page into each week’s management meeting: what came in, where every receivable stands, which exceptions are open and who owns them, and the decisions management must make. Every figure ties to the aging report.
- **[Year-end tax packet for the accountant](year-end-tax-packet-for-the-accountant/)**: Once the year's books are closed, the owner or bookkeeper hands the accountant one packet: the documents asked for and what is still missing, every contractor paid over the reporting line with missing tax forms flagged, the year's estimated tax payments, and the questions to ask. It works out no tax.

## How to use one

Open a folder and start with its README. Every folder stands alone, so copy the one you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini: paste the instruction block and the output format in as custom instructions, or load the folder in a product that reads `SKILL.md` packages.

To take the whole collection, press **Use this template** at the top of this page: it copies every folder into a repository in your own account, yours to change.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## What SKILL.md is

Each folder holds a file named `SKILL.md`. It is the technical file: the same instructions and output format as the template, in the packaged form that AI products which load instructions from a folder read. It is generated from `TEMPLATE.md`, so the two always agree. The workflow template is the whole folder; `SKILL.md` is one way of loading it. If your AI product does not read that format, use `TEMPLATE.md`.

## What "checked" means here

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and whether it has been tried on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Test a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
