# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The shape of the work comes from the payroll preparation method in the small-business plugin of anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0). From its main instructions: fix the pay period, pay date and roster first; use the pay rate held in the payroll record rather than one written on a timesheet; leave a missing punch unknown rather than guess it; flag every oddity for the owner and fix nothing on its own; compare with the last run and explain a move above 10%; put each flag to the owner on its own, with what happens if it is left; state headcount, hours, gross, pay date and open flags before approval; and leave submitting the run to the owner. From its anomaly rules: the checks and their default limits (a shift over 12 hours, a day over 16, seven days in a row, overtime above 125% of a person's four-run average, hours 10% over the schedule, a near-zero shift, overlapping entries flagged and not combined), wording each flag with who, when, both figures and the cost or an unknown cost, and putting people with open questions first. From its timesheet intake: asking which break convention a sheet uses, recomputing totalled sheets from the punches, no rounding without a stated rule, and checking the roster for hours before a start date, someone missing since the last run and a name with no record. From its run sheet format: flags attached to each person, saying what a gross leaves out, never estimating employer taxes, and a cash line that says plainly when no balance was given. From its list of common mistakes: asking once whether breaks are already taken out, applying the answer throughout and saying on the sheet which way was used, asking about a rate change rather than accepting it, and keeping a name with no record out of the run. From the plugin's shared rule on untrusted content: a request to change bank details is quoted and verified through a channel the business already holds. The source works through live payroll connections and stages the run in the payroll system; this version works only from exports a person supplies, reads them by stated column headers and stops when one does not match, applies only the overtime rule the company supplies and makes no statement on tax or employment law, lists everything kept out of the totals, and keys in, submits and pays nothing. No third-party text is reused.
